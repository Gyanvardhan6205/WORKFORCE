About Us

In today’s world, everything is going online whether it’s booking a taxi or ordering food. Now a days, order could be made via just some clicks on smart phones and wanted things are obtained at the door steps. People get things done at their doorstep.

But there are few services which are still offline at tier-2 & tier 3 cities like Muzffarpur ,Kanpur,Paryagraj etc. One of them is hiring a worker like plumber, electrician, etc. One has to roam here and there to find the desired workers.

In our society, people are facing problems in finding and hiring local technical professionals to avail repairing and maintenance services for their homes and offices. Due to rapid advancements in technology, the smart phones are proved to be an imperative source of communication and now have become an integral part of our daily lives.

In today’s application-friendly environment people are seeking for the job and consumers are looking out for services directly via smart phones. Mobile industry is now a major source of employment opportunities for us

Our Website facilitate to communicate with the user and expert. It contains Google map and feature to navigate the current location so that user can search and hire the local professionals easily.

It is a contribution towards solution for local workers who are unaware of the power of smart phones.
